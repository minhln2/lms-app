Tense changes · lùi thì

Kể lại bằng said, told, asked thì động từ lùi về quá khứ một bậc: is → was, like → liked, will → would, has done → had done.

📈 Dòng thời gian

📏 Quy tắc

1. Hiện tại → quá khứ: am / is / are → was / were; V(s) → V2; am / is / are + V-ing → was / were + V-ing

  • Lan said she lived near the park. — Lời gốc: "I live near the park."
  • Nam said he was watching TV. — Lời gốc: "I'm watching TV."

Present Simple → Past Simple; Present Continuous → Past Continuous. Câu bị động cũng lùi như vậy: is cleaned → was cleaned (Passive forms (tenses, modals, to, -ing)).

2. Quá khứ đơn và hiện tại hoàn thành → had + V3 (Past Perfect)

  • Tom said he had lost his pencil. — Lời gốc: "I lost my pencil."
  • They said they had finished the puzzle. — Lời gốc: "We've finished the puzzle."

Past Simple, Present Perfect → Past Perfect. have been + V-ing → had been + V-ing. was / were + V-ing thường giữ nguyên (khi đổi thì thành had been + V-ing). Nói hằng ngày, V2 hay được giữ nguyên: Tom said he lost his pencil.

3. will → would, can → could, may → might, must → had to; am / is going to → was / were going to

  • Mai said she would help me. — Lời gốc: "I'll help you."
  • Bin said he could swim. — Lời gốc: "I can swim."
  • Mom said I had to be home by six. — Lời gốc: "You must be home by six."

must không có dạng quá khứ nên mượn had to → must & have to. Nghĩa của từng modal → Modal verbs.

4. Đã lùi hết cỡ thì giữ nguyên: had + V3, would, could, might, should, used to

  • The doctor said I should rest. — Lời gốc: "You should rest."
  • Grandpa said he used to walk to school. — Lời gốc: "I used to walk to school."

ought to, had better, mustn't cũng giữ nguyên.

5. Câu điều kiện: loại 1 lùi thành loại 2 (if + V2, would + V); loại 2 và loại 3 giữ nguyên

  • Dad said if it rained, we would stay home. — Lời gốc: "If it rains, we'll stay home."
  • Lan said if she had wings, she would fly to school. — Lời gốc: "If I had wings, I would fly to school." — loại 2, giữ nguyên.

Loại 1 lùi đúng như luật thường: rains → rained, will → would. Loại 2 và loại 3 đã ở dạng lùi hết cỡ.

6. Không cần lùi: động từ tường thuật ở hiện tại (says, tells), hoặc điều nói ra vẫn còn đúng

  • Mom says dinner is ready. — Mẹ vừa bảo — chuyển lời ngay, giữ is.
  • Our teacher said the Earth goes around the Sun. — Sự thật lúc nào cũng đúng → giữ goes.

Động từ tường thuật ở tương lai hay hiện tại hoàn thành cũng không lùi: Lan has said she is coming. Điều vẫn còn đúng thì lùi hay giữ đều được; điều đã đổi khác thì phải lùi.

⚠ Lỗi hay gặp

✗ Last week Kim said she will call me.

✓ Last week Kim said she would call me.

Kể lại lời đã qua bằng said → will lùi thành would.

✗ Mom says dinner was ready.

✓ Mom says dinner is ready.

says ở hiện tại → giữ nguyên thì, không lùi.

✗ Tom said he had went home.

✓ Tom said he had gone home.

Sau had là V3 (go → went → gone) — tra ở Irregular verbs.
