Reported statements · tường thuật câu kể

Kể lại lời ai đó đã nói, không trích nguyên văn: S + said (that) + câu đã backshift một bậc. Chữ that bỏ được.

🧮 Công thức

+
S + said (that) + S + V (backshift) Nam said (that) he was hungry.
+
S + told + sb + (that) + S + V (backshift) Lan told me she had a new puppy.
−
S + said (that) + S + didn't / wasn't / couldn't + V Mai said she didn't like spinach.
?
What did + S + say? What did the coach say?
→ He said we were ready for the game.

🧭 Cách dùng

1. Kể lại lời ai đó đã nói: said (that) + câu đã backshift một bậc

  • Nam said (that) he was hungry. — Direct speech: "I'm hungry." — Nam nói là bạn ấy đói.
  • Mom said she would pick us up at five. — Direct speech: "I'll pick you up at five."

Cách backshift: Backshift: tense changes. Đổi I → he, my → his: Pronoun changes.

2. Muốn nêu cả người nghe: told + person + (that)…

  • Lan told me (that) she had a new puppy. — Direct speech: "I have a new puppy." — Lan kể với tớ.
  • Ben told his mom he was sorry. — Direct speech: "I'm sorry, Mom."

said không đi thẳng với người: said me ✗, told me ✓. Chọn reporting verb: Reporting verbs: say, tell & ask.

3. Negative sentence: backshift ở auxiliary — don't → didn't, can't → couldn't, won't → wouldn't

  • Mai said she didn't like spinach. — Direct speech: "I don't like spinach."
  • Bin said he couldn't find his shoes. — Direct speech: "I can't find my shoes."

4. Chuyển lời vừa nghe, hoặc điều vẫn còn đúng: says (that) + giữ nguyên tense

  • Mom says dinner is ready. — Mẹ bảo cơm xong rồi. (vừa nói, chuyển lời ngay)
  • Our teacher says we have a test on Friday. — Cô bảo thứ Sáu tụi mình có bài kiểm tra.

Reporting verb ở Present (says, tells) thì không backshift — Backshift: tense changes.

🔑 Dấu hiệu nhận biết

said (that)told me (that)says (that)

⚠ Lỗi hay gặp

✗ Last week Kim said she will call me.

✓ Last week Kim said she would call me.

Lời nói đã qua, kể lại bằng said → will lùi thành would.

✗ She said me that she was tired.

✓ She told me that she was tired.

Có người nghe ngay sau verb → told + person.

✗ Tom said that "I am hungry."

✓ Tom said that he was hungry.

Đã có that thì bỏ ngoặc kép, đổi I → he và backshift.
