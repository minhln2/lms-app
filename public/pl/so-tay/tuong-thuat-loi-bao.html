Reported commands & requests · tường thuật mệnh lệnh & lời nhờ

Kể lại lời bảo, lời nhờ: told / asked + người + (not) to + V. Động từ sau to giữ nguyên mẫu, không phải lùi thì.

🧮 Công thức

+
S + told / asked + người + to + V Mom told me to clean my room.
−
S + told / asked + người + not to + V The teacher told us not to run in the hall.
?
What did + S + tell / ask + người + to do? What did the coach tell you to do?
→ He told us to run three laps.

🧭 Cách dùng

1. Mệnh lệnh, lời dặn: told + người + to + V

  • Mom told me to clean my room. — Lời gốc: "Clean your room!"
  • The coach told us to drink some water. — Lời gốc: "Drink some water."

Lời khuyên, lời nhắc cũng theo mẫu này: advised / reminded / warned + người + to + V.

2. Lời nhờ, lời xin (có please, Can you…?): asked + người + to + V

  • Lan asked me to help her with her math. — Lời gốc: "Can you help me with my math, please?"
  • I asked Dad to buy some apples. — Lời gốc: "Please buy some apples, Dad."

Cũng nói được: Lan asked if I could help her — theo mẫu Reported questions.

3. Bảo đừng làm (Don't…): not to + V

  • The teacher told us not to run in the hall. — Lời gốc: "Don't run in the hall!"
  • Mai asked me not to tell anyone. — Lời gốc: "Please don't tell anyone."

not đứng TRƯỚC to: not to run — không viết don't run hay to not run.

🔑 Dấu hiệu nhận biết

told … toasked … tonot to

⚠ Lỗi hay gặp

✗ Mom told me clean my room.

✓ Mom told me to clean my room.

Sau told / asked + người là to + V.

✗ The teacher told us don't run.

✓ The teacher told us not to run.

Bảo đừng làm → not to + V, bỏ don't.

✗ Lan asked to me to help her.

✓ Lan asked me to help her.

ask + người, không có to: asked me, asked Dad.

✗ Mom said me to clean my room.

✓ Mom told me to clean my room.

Lời bảo dùng told / asked + người + to V, không dùng said → say, tell & ask.
