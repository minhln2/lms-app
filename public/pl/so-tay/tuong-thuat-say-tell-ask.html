Reporting verbs: say, tell & ask · chọn động từ tường thuật

say (that) không cần người nghe; tell PHẢI có người nghe ngay sau (tell me, tell Mom); ask dùng cho question và request.

📏 Quy tắc

1. say (that) — không cần nêu người nghe; muốn nêu thì say to + person

  • Nam said (that) he was tired. — Nam nói là bạn ấy mệt.
  • Nam said to me that he was tired. — Nam nói với tớ là bạn ấy mệt.

2. tell + person + (that) — tell me, tell us, tell Mom

  • Nam told me (that) he was tired. — Nam bảo tớ là bạn ấy mệt.
  • Mom told us to wash our hands. — Mẹ bảo tụi tớ đi rửa tay.

Ngoại lệ: tell a story · tell a joke · tell the truth · tell a lie

Mấy cụm trên đi với tell mà không cần người nghe: Grandpa told a funny story.

3. ask (+ person) — cho question và request

  • She asked me where I lived. — Bạn ấy hỏi tớ sống ở đâu.
  • She asked me to close the window. — Bạn ấy nhờ tớ đóng cửa sổ.

Question: Reported questions. Request: Reported commands & requests. ask + person, không có to: asked me ✓, asked to me ✗.

4. Reporting verb khác cho rõ nghĩa: explain, reply, promise, admit, agree, complain + (that) — dùng như say

  • Dad promised (that) he would come to my game. — Bố hứa sẽ tới xem tớ thi đấu.
  • She explained that the bus was late. — Bạn ấy giải thích là xe buýt tới muộn.

Viết truyện có direct speech thì thay said bằng shouted, whispered, laughed… — Direct speech.

5. offer, promise, agree, refuse + to V — kể lại lời đề nghị, lời hứa, đồng ý, từ chối

  • Ben offered to carry my bag. — Direct speech: "I'll carry your bag." — Ben ngỏ ý xách giúp.
  • My little brother refused to eat his carrots. — Direct speech: "I won't eat them!" — em tớ không chịu ăn.

Không có người nghe chen giữa: offered to carry ✓, offered me to carry ✗. Các verb + to V khác → Verb + to V.

6. advise, remind, warn, invite, encourage + person + to V

  • Mom reminded me to take my lunch. — Direct speech: "Don't forget your lunch!"
  • Kim invited us to come to her party. — Direct speech: "Would you like to come to my party?"

Cùng mẫu với told / asked + person + (not) to V → Reported commands & requests. Danh sách verb + person + to V → Verb + person + to V.

7. suggest, admit, deny + V-ing; apologize for + V-ing; accuse + person + of + V-ing

  • Lan suggested going to the park. — Direct speech: "Let's go to the park!"
  • Tom apologized for breaking the vase. — Direct speech: "I'm sorry I broke the vase."
  • Bin denied eating the last cookie. — Direct speech: "I didn't eat it!" — Bin chối.

Dùng verb + V-ing (suggested going ✓, suggested to go ✗) → Verb + V-ing; preposition + V-ing → Preposition + V-ing.

⚠ Lỗi hay gặp

✗ She said me that she was sick.

✓ She told me that she was sick.

Có người nghe ngay sau verb → tell.

✗ He told that he was busy.

✓ He said that he was busy.

tell phải có người nghe (He told me that…); không nêu người thì dùng say.

✗ I asked to Mom for help.

✓ I asked Mom for help.

ask + person ngay sau, không có to.

✗ Lan suggested to go to the park.

✓ Lan suggested going to the park.

suggest + V-ing, không + to V.
