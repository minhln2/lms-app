V-ing: where to use it · những chỗ dùng V-ing

Gồm:Verb + V-inglike, start… + to V or V-ingremember, stop, try… + to V or V-ingsee, hear + person + V / V-ingV-ing as subject

V-ing gặp ở nhiều chỗ: sau enjoy / finish, làm subject, sau be, sau preposition.
Sau preposition luôn dùng V-ing, kể cả preposition to trong look forward to, be used to.
Đừng nhầm với việc đang làm: My hobby is drawing (sở thích) khác I'm drawing (đang vẽ).

🃏 Tóm tắt

Verb + V-ing

Sau enjoy, finish, keep, avoid, mind, suggest, practice… verb thứ hai là V-ing.
V-ing cũng dùng trong go swimming, spend time reading, can't help laughing.

I enjoy reading comics before bed.

like, start… + to V or V-ing

like, love, hate, prefer, start, begin, continue đi được cả to V lẫn V-ing, nghĩa gần như nhau. Nhưng would like / would love chỉ đi với to V.

It started to rain.

remember, stop, try… + to V or V-ing

remember, forget, stop, try, regret, go on: đổi to V ↔ V-ing là đổi nghĩa. Mẹo: V-ing = việc đã / đang có; to V = việc tới sau, việc nhắm tới.

I remember locking the door.

see, hear + person + V / V-ing

see / hear / watch / feel + person + V-ing — thấy một lúc khi việc đang diễn ra

I heard Mom singing in the kitchen.

V-ing as subject

V-ing dùng được như một noun: đứng đầu câu làm subject (Swimming is fun) hay sau be (My hobby is drawing). Main verb ở dạng singular.

Swimming is my favorite sport.

👯 Dễ nhầm

I hope to see you soon.

hope + to V — to đi với verb → Verb + to V.

I look forward to seeing you.

to ở đây là preposition → V-ing → Preposition + V-ing.

I used to walk to school.

Ngày xưa hay đi bộ, giờ thôi → used to & would.

I'm used to walking to school.

Đã QUEN đi bộ rồi → be used to & get used to.

My hobby is drawing.

Sở thích là GÌ — V-ing như một noun → V-ing as subject.

I'm drawing a cat right now.

ĐANG làm lúc này → Present Continuous.

This game is boring.

Adjective: trò chơi GÂY chán → -ed & -ing adjectives.

⚠ Lỗi lẫn lộn

✗ I'm good at draw.

✓ I'm good at drawing.

Sau preposition là V-ing → Preposition + V-ing.

✗ After eat lunch, we played tag.

✓ After eating lunch, we played tag.

after, before, without + V-ing → Preposition + V-ing.

✗ I look forward to see you.

✓ I look forward to seeing you.

to trong look forward to là preposition → V-ing → Preposition + V-ing.

✗ I'm used to get up early.

✓ I'm used to getting up early.

be used to = đã quen; to là preposition → V-ing → be used to & get used to.
