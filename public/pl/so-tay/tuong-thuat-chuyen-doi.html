Direct → reported speech: tenses, pronouns, time & place · lời trực tiếp → tường thuật

Gồm:Direct speechBackshift: tense changesPronoun changesTime & place changes

Tense lùi một bậc (backshift): is → was, will → would, has done → had done.
Pronoun đổi theo người được nhắc tới: I → he / she, my → his / her, you → I.
Thời gian & nơi chốn đổi nếu lúc kể khác ngày, khác chỗ: tomorrow → the next day.

🃏 Tóm tắt

Direct speech

Chép NGUYÊN lời người nói trong ngoặc kép "…"; dấu phẩy, chấm, ?, ! nằm TRONG ngoặc. Không trích nguyên văn thì dùng reported speech.

"I'm hungry," said Nam.

Backshift: tense changes

Backshift: khi kể lại bằng said, told, asked, verb lùi một bậc về Past — is → was, like → liked, will → would, has done → had done.

Lan said she lived near the park.

Pronoun changes

Khi kể lại, đổi pronoun (I, my, we, you…) theo đúng người được nhắc tới: "I love my bike," Tom said → Tom said he loved his bike.

Tom said he loved his bike.

Time & place changes

Lúc kể đã KHÁC ngày, KHÁC chỗ thì đổi: today → that day, tomorrow → the next day, here → there. Cùng ngày, cùng chỗ thì giữ.

Lan said she was busy that day.

👯 Từ direct speech sang reported speech

"I am tired," said Nam.

Direct speech — nguyên văn.

Nam said he was tired.

Backshift: am → was.

"I lost my key," said Mai.

Lời của Mai.

Mai said she had lost her key.

Đổi pronoun: I → she, my → her (và backshift).

"We'll go to the zoo tomorrow," said Dad.

Bố nói hôm thứ Bảy.

Dad said we would go to the zoo the next day.

Kể lại hôm thứ Hai: tomorrow → the next day.

"Dinner is ready!" Mom calls.

Mẹ vừa gọi.

Mom says dinner is ready.

Chuyển lời ngay, says ở Present → giữ is.

⚠ Lỗi lẫn lộn

✗ Mai said that "I lost my key."

✓ Mai said that she had lost her key.

Có that thì bỏ ngoặc kép, rồi đổi pronoun và backshift.

✗ Mai said she had lost my key.

✓ Mai said she had lost her key.

my là của Mai → her → Pronoun changes.

✗ On Saturday Dad said we would go to the zoo tomorrow.

✓ On Saturday Dad said we would go to the zoo the next day.

Kể lại vào ngày khác → tomorrow thành the next day → Time & place changes.
