Time & place words · đổi từ chỉ thời gian & nơi chốn

Lúc kể đã KHÁC ngày, KHÁC chỗ thì đổi: today → that day, tomorrow → the next day, here → there. Cùng ngày, cùng chỗ thì giữ.

📏 Quy tắc

1. Lúc nói: now → then, today → that day, tonight → that night, this morning → that morning, at the moment → at that moment

  • Lan said she was busy that day. — Lời gốc: "I'm busy today."
  • Nam said he was reading at that moment. — Lời gốc: "I'm reading at the moment."

this + mốc thời gian đổi thành that: this week → that week, this year → that year.

2. Trước đó: yesterday → the day before, last week → the week before, ago → before

  • Mai said she had seen the movie the day before. — Lời gốc: "I saw the movie yesterday."
  • Bin said he had moved there two years before. — Lời gốc: "I moved here two years ago."

the day before = the previous day; the week before = the previous week.

3. Sau đó: tomorrow → the next day, next week → the following week

  • Dad said we would go camping the next day. — Lời gốc: "We'll go camping tomorrow."
  • Kim said her party was the following week. — Lời gốc: "My party is next week."

the next day = the following day.

4. Nơi chốn, chỉ trỏ: here → there, this → that, these → those

  • He said my books were there on the table. — Lời gốc: "Your books are here on the table."
  • Lan said that was her favorite song. — Lời gốc: "This is my favorite song."

5. Kể ngay trong ngày, ngay tại chỗ thì giữ nguyên

  • Nam said he would come tomorrow. — Sáng nay Nam nói vậy — tomorrow vẫn là ngày mai.
  • The teacher said we could sit here. — Cả lớp vẫn đang ở chỗ đó.

Tự hỏi: lúc kể, "today" còn là hôm nay không? Còn thì giữ.

⚠ Lỗi hay gặp

✗ Last Monday, Nam said he would come tomorrow.

✓ Last Monday, Nam said he would come the next day.

Lời nói đã qua nhiều ngày → tomorrow thành the next day.
