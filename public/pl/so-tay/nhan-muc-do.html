Degree words: very, so, such, too & enough · mức độ

Gồm:Types & position of adverbsso & suchtoo & enough

very chỉ nói mức độ cao, đứng trước adjective — very warm.
so và such a nghĩa là 'thật là…!': so + adjective (so cute), such a + adjective + noun.
too là quá, không làm được (too hot to eat); enough là đủ, đứng sau adjective (warm enough).

🃏 Tóm tắt

Types & position of adverbs

Degree word (very, really, pretty, quite, a bit, too, so): đứng TRƯỚC adjective hoặc adverb — riêng enough đứng SAU

The test was really easy.

so & such

Nhấn mạnh: so + adjective / adverb (so cute); such + (a/an) + adjective + noun (such a cute dog). Thêm that… để nói kết quả.

Your puppy is so cute!

too & enough

too + adjective = QUÁ, đến mức không được (too cold to swim); adjective + enough = ĐỦ (old enough). enough đứng SAU adjective, TRƯỚC noun.

This shirt is too small for me.

👯 very hay too?

This box is very heavy, but I can carry it.

Rất nặng — vẫn bê được.

This box is too heavy. I can't carry it.

Nặng quá — không bê nổi.

This cake is so sweet! I love it.

Ngọt ghê — khen.

This cake is too sweet. I can't eat it.

Ngọt quá — chê.

Types & position of adverbs

The pizza is very hot. — Chỉ tả: rất nóng.

so & such

Wow, it smells so good! — Exclamation, khen.

so & such

It's such a big pizza! — Exclamation, có noun pizza → such a.

too & enough

It's too hot to eat right now. — Nóng quá, chưa ăn được.

too & enough

Is it cool enough to eat now? — Đã đủ nguội để ăn chưa?

⚠ Lỗi lẫn lộn

✗ It's very hot to eat.

✓ It's too hot to eat.

Nóng đến mức không ăn được → too … to, không dùng very.

✗ It was so funny movie.

✓ It was such a funny movie.

Có noun movie → such a → so & such.

✗ I was very tired that I fell asleep.

✓ I was so tired that I fell asleep.

Nói kết quả "đến nỗi" → so … that, không dùng very.
